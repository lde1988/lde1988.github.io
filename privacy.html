Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Legal Vault is a private tool used by one person, its owner, on the owner's own Google accounts. It is not available to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it reads email in the owner's own Gmail account. It has read-only access (the gmail.readonly permission). It cannot send, change or delete email.

What it does with the data

It copies selected emails, those to or from a list of addresses the owner chooses, into the owner's private archive. The archive is kept on the owner's own computer and the owner's private server.

The data is used only by the owner, for the owner's personal records. It is not sold or shared with anyone, and it is not used for advertising or to train AI models.

Google user data

Legal Vault's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Withdrawing it stops all further reading of email.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the developer contact email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
